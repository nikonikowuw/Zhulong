AXIS Object Analytics | Technical Documentation

help.axis.com/en-us/axis-object-analytics
AXIS Object Analytics detects, classifies, and counts moving objects, especially humans or vehicles. You can set up scenarios with conditions, such as objects that move or stay longer than a set time within a defined area or that cross a defined line. When the app detects or counts objects, your Axis devices or third-party software can record video, play an audio message, or alert security staff.
